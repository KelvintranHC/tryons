    06FAQ

    Have a question?

    What clients often ask Wave before starting a project.

    What types of products and brands do you work with?

    Fashion, beauty, jewelry, interior, lifestyle, and more. If your brand needs visual storytelling — from a single product image to a full campaign film — we can build the right workflow.

    How does the production process work?

    We follow a structured pipeline: creative direction, concept & storyboard, AI visual production, character direction, cinematic animation, and final film assembly. One product, one team, one process.

    What do I need to prepare before we start?

    A product image, basic brand references, and a clear goal for the film — launch, campaign, or lookbook. No complex production brief required. We help shape the story from there.

    How long does a typical project take?

    Timeline depends on scope — a single film, a campaign set, or a full collection rollout. After the brief, we share a clear schedule with milestones before production begins.

    How is this different from traditional production?

    You get cinematic quality without organizing a large traditional shoot. We combine brand thinking, artistic direction, and AI production — faster iteration, lower overhead, same creative control.

    Can agencies and creative teams collaborate with Wave?

    Yes. We work as a production partner — expanding your visual capacity, testing concepts quickly, and delivering campaign-ready content your team can present to clients.
